<h2 class="has-text-align-center">Nature is Binaural </h2>
<!-- /wp:heading --></div>
<!-- /wp:column -->

<!-- wp:column {"width":"20%"} -->
<div class="wp-block-column" style="flex-basis:20%"></div>

<!-- wp:image {"align":"wide","id":103,"sizeSlug":"large","linkDestination":"none"} -->
<figure class="wp-block-image alignwide size-large"><img src="https://nailbright.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/12/cover-642x1024.jpg" alt="" class="wp-image-103"/></figure>
<!-- /wp:image -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"customFontSize":15} -->
<p style="font-size:15px"><strong>Introducing Bi Natural Beats, a unique album that combines the beauty of nature with binaural beats. Our tracks are carefully crafted using real-life recordings from the Rocky Mountains and specifically tuned brain waves to achieve a range of desired states of consciousness. With four tracks to choose from, there's something for every mood and situation.</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:html -->
<figure class="wp-block-audio"></audio><figcaption class="wp-element-caption">Rockin Robin</figcaption><audio controls src="https://binaturalbeats.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/12/RockinRobn.mp3" controlsList="nodownload"></figure>
<!-- /wp:html -->

<!-- wp:preformatted -->
<pre class="wp-block-preformatted">Track 1, Rockin' Robbin, is perfect for your daily tasks. It's tuned to 741 hz to promote problem-solving and puts your brain into beta. This track features the sounds of local birds in Boulder, CO, and is sure to help you focus and stay on track.</pre>
<!-- /wp:preformatted -->

<!-- wp:html -->
<figure class="wp-block-audio"><figcaption class="wp-element-caption">Awakened</figcaption><audio controls="" src="https://binaturalbeats.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/12/11_awakened.mp3" controlslist="nodownload"></audio></figure>
<!-- /wp:html -->

<!-- wp:preformatted -->
<pre class="wp-block-preformatted">Track 2, Awakened, is a special recording of Elk bugling all night in Rocky Mountain National Park. This track is tuned to 852 hz to promote returning to spiritual order and puts your brain into the Theta and Delta state. It's the perfect track for meditation and reconnecting with yourself.</pre>
<!-- /wp:preformatted -->

<!-- wp:html -->
<figure class="wp-block-audio"><figcaption class="wp-element-caption">Rainy Daze </figcaption><audio controls="" src="https://binaturalbeats.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/12/12_ranydaze.mp3" controlslist="nodownload"></audio></figure>
<!-- /wp:html -->

<!-- wp:preformatted -->
<pre class="wp-block-preformatted">Track 3, Rainy Daze, is a soothing and relaxing track recorded on a rainy evening in the Rocky Mountains. It's tuned to 396 hz to promote the release of fear, guilt, and anxiety and puts your brain into the Alpha and Theta states. This track is excellent for meditation or as background noise while you unwind.</pre>
<!-- /wp:preformatted -->

<!-- wp:html -->
<figure class="wp-block-audio"><figcaption class="wp-element-caption">Sleepy Elk</figcaption><audio controls="" src="https://binaturalbeats.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/12/13_sleepyElk-1.mp3" controlslist="nodownload"></audio></figure>
<!-- /wp:html -->

<!-- wp:preformatted -->
<pre class="wp-block-preformatted">Track 4, Sleepy Elk, is the perfect way to end your day. It's tuned to 174 Hz to promote deep relaxation, slowly bringing your brain from Alpha to Delta and putting you into a peaceful sleep. This track was recorded next to a babbling brook in the Rocky Mountains, with the sounds of Elk in the background.</pre>
<!-- /wp:preformatted -->
